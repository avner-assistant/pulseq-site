PulseQ is a systematic trading firm based in Tel Aviv. We research, test and run many independent trading strategies side by side, and every order passes automated risk checks before capital moves.

How we work

  • Research

    We turn market ideas into exact rules and test them on data the rule has never seen before any money goes in.

  • Portfolio

    We run different strategies together and adjust how much money each receives as market conditions change.

  • Risk and execution

    Position and loss limits are enforced in code, and a person can step in at any time.

  • Technology

    Our research, testing, portfolio and trading tools are built in-house.
